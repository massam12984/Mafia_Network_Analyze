Step 02 — The Science

How We Find
The Boss

01
Load Network

Your CSV is parsed with Pandas and converted into a NetworkX graph representing connections between entities.

02
Degree Centrality

Measures how many direct connections a node has. High degree = many contacts in the organisation.

03
Betweenness

Counts how often a node sits on shortest paths. High betweenness = information broker and gatekeeper.

04
Eigenvector

A node is influential if its neighbours are influential. Eigenvector centrality captures prestige and elite connections.

05
Boss Score

Final Score = 0.3×Degree + 0.3×Betweenness + 0.4×Eigenvector. Highest score reveals the network's commander.
